# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and
professional community.

Participants are expected to:

-   Be respectful.
-   Assume good faith.
-   Give constructive feedback.
-   Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
-   Respect different backgrounds and experience levels.
-   Keep security discussions responsible and private when appropriate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing,
deliberate disruption, and malicious disclosure of private information
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this
code.

For serious or sensitive concerns, contact the project maintainers
privately.
